# Crew Lodging in Altoona: A Checklist for Contractors and Coordinators

A crew booking works best when the coordinator and lodging operator agree on the rooming list, assignment dates and payment responsibilities before anyone travels. A few clear details prevent confusion at arrival and when the job schedule changes.

## Send exact dates and room needs

Include check-in and check-out dates, the number of adult workers, preferred room arrangement and the jobsite area. Tell the operator about shifts or unusual arrival times. Do not assume that seating, a couch or empty floor space counts as an approved bed. Each offered room needs a verified capacity, and a multiple-room quote should identify the physical rooms it includes.

## Ask how shared spaces work

Turn Key STR is planned as owner-occupied furnished lodging with shared household areas. The quote should explain bathroom access, quiet hours, permitted appliances, storage and housekeeping. Booking all available guest rooms does not automatically mean exclusive use of an entire house. If a crew needs that arrangement, it must be specifically offered and approved.

## Agree on billing before arrival

Provide the company's billing contact and any purchase-order reference. Ask whether card, invoice or ACH payment is available for the approved account. A purchase order, an unanswered quote or a pending payment does not by itself reserve inventory. The quote needs an expiry time, amount due, balance date and a clear confirmation process. List any security deposit separately from the lodging total.

## Confirm vehicles, laundry and arrival logistics

Tell the operator the number and size of work vehicles. A paid space must actually be available and suitable for the vehicle. Discuss workwear and special-soil laundry needs with the laundry service before sending items. Ask about pickup and return schedules, and confirm any transport request separately. These services should appear as approved items rather than assumed inclusions.

## Plan changes and substitutions

Jobs move, workers rotate and assignments extend. Ask who may authorize a name change, additional traveler or extension. New occupants need approval and the required ID verification. A later week must be checked against the same room calendar used for every other reservation. There is no automatic renewal of a sold-out room.

Use a secure application or verification service for sensitive information. A coordinator should not send copies of every worker's ID or payment card through ordinary email. Each traveler needs to receive the applicable house rules and arrival instructions.

Send a [corporate quote request](/corporate-quote) with your dates and rooming needs. The written proposal should make the room, services, accepted price and reservation status clear to both the coordinator and the travelers.
